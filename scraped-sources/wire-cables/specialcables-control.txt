                                                                          CONTROL CABLES
Application: Mainly used for interconnections for control circuits, communication systems, electrical panels,
machine tools as well as lighting at lower loads.

Types and Sizes: Typically upto 61 cores from 1.0 sq. mm to 2.5 sq. mm with voltage grade upto 1.1 KV

Conductor: Copper – Solid, Stranded (Circular) or Flexible – Bare or Tinned

Insulation: PVC- GP/HR/FR/FRLS, PE, XLPE, Zero Halogen or EPR Rubber.

Inner Sheath: PVC - GP/HR/FR/FRLS, PE, Zero Halogen or Rubber – FR/HOFR

Armour: Galvanized Steel Round Wire / Flat Strip / Tape or Aluminium Round Wire / Flat Strip or GI / Tinned Copper Braiding

Outer Sheath: PVC - GP/HR/FR/FRLS, PE, Zero Halogen or Rubber- FR/HOFR/TRS.

Specification: Generally confirming to IS: 694, IS: 1554 (Pt-I), IS: 7098 (Pt-I), IEC: 60502-1, BS: 5467, BS: 6346

Key Features:
1.   Provides high degree of protection against electrical interference
2.   Cable design can be customised to meet customer requirements
3.   Allows better transmission of power and signal at very low voltages to control processes and critical
     equipment with low effect of induced voltage.
