Answers to all your possible questions.

What is the difference between VX and AX gaskets?

VX gaskets are designed for face-to-face sealing between hubs, while AX gaskets are used for flange-to-flange sealing.

Do you provide technical support for selecting the right VX or AX gasket?

Absolutely! Our knowledgeable team is available to assist you in selecting the appropriate VX or AX gasket for your specific application. Please contact us if you need expert advice.

Can VX and AX gaskets be customized to fit specific dimensions?

Yes, we provide customization options for VX and AX gaskets to ensure a perfect fit for your specific requirements. Contact us with your dimensional specifications for a personalized solution.

How long do VX and AX gaskets last?

The lifespan of VX and AX gaskets depends on various factors, such as operating conditions, application, and maintenance. Generally, with proper installation and regular inspection, they can provide long-lasting sealing performance.
