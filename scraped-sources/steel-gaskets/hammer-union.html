Hammer Union Manufacturer for High-Pressure Oilfield Applications

Hammer unions are high-pressure pipe connectors used in oil and gas drilling, well servicing, and industrial fluid transfer systems. Designed for quick connection and disconnection without specialized tools, hammer unions provide a secure, leak-proof seal in high-pressure and high-vibration environments. They are widely used in mud lines, cementing lines, fracturing lines, manifolds, and flowline systems where safety and pressure integrity are critical.

At Asian Sealing Products, we manufacture premium-quality hammer unions using high-strength forged steel, engineered to meet demanding oilfield service conditions. Each hammer union consists of three precision-machined components: the wing nut, male sub, and female sub. When assembled, these components create a tight, pressure-resistant metal-to-metal seal capable of handling up to 20,000 PSI cold working pressure.

Our hammer union are available in sizes from 1 inch to 12 inches and are suitable for standard service as well as sour gas applications. We strictly follow industry standards, including API RP 14E and NACE MR-01-75 to ensure corrosion resistance, operational safety, and long-term reliability in harsh environments. Whether you require standard figure hammer union or customized solutions for high-pressure oilfield operations, we deliver durable, performance-driven connections built for extreme conditions.

Sealing Design Types

Hammer unions are available with different sealing designs based on pressure rating and service conditions, ensuring leak-proof performance in oilfield and high-pressure fluid systems.

Low-Pressure Hammer Union
 Designed with a metal-to-metal seal formed by precision-machined conical and spherical surfaces. Suitable for standard pressure applications where reliable sealing and quick connection are required.

Medium-Pressure Hammer Union
 Features a metal-to-metal seal combined with an additional O-ring to enhance sealing integrity. This dual-seal design improves leak resistance under moderate pressure conditions.

High-Pressure Hammer Union
 Utilizes a lip-type seal ring positioned inside the female union to provide superior sealing performance. Engineered for high-pressure oilfield applications, this design ensures maximum pressure containment and operational safety.

Maintenance on Hammer Unions

Proper maintenance of hammer unions is critical to ensure safe operation, pressure integrity, and long service life in high-pressure oil and gas applications. Regular inspection and preventive care help avoid leaks, equipment failure, and costly downtime.

Inspection & Wear Check
 Periodically inspect the wing nut, male sub, and female sub for wear, corrosion, cracks, or deformation. Damaged components should be replaced immediately to maintain sealing performance.

Sealing Surface Care
 Keep metal-to-metal sealing surfaces clean and free from dirt, rust, or debris. Even minor contamination can compromise the seal and cause leakage under pressure.

Thread Lubrication
 Apply suitable anti-seize compound to threads and contact areas to prevent galling and ensure smooth assembly and disassembly, especially in high-pressure service conditions.

Seal & Alignment Verification
 Before reinstallation, check for thread damage, proper alignment, and seal integrity to ensure correct engagement and secure pressure containment.

Routine hammer union maintenance enhances operational safety, reduces unplanned shutdowns, and extends equipment lifespan in demanding oilfield environments.

Threads and contact areas should be lightly lubricated with appropriate anti-seize compounds to prevent galling and ease repeated assembly and disassembly. It is also recommended to check for thread damage, misalignment, and seal integrity before reinstallation. 

Routine maintenance ensures maximum reliability, minimizes downtime, and extends the overall service life of hammer unions in demanding operating environments.

Answers to all your possible questions.

What is a hammer union used for?

A hammer union is a high-pressure pipe connection used in oil and gas drilling, cementing, fracturing, and well servicing operations. It allows quick assembly and disassembly of flowlines, manifolds, and high-pressure piping systems while maintaining a secure, leak-proof seal.

What pressure ratings are available for hammer unions?

Hammer unions are available in various pressure ratings ranging from low-pressure models to high-pressure designs up to 20,000 PSI cold working pressure, depending on the figure type and material grade.

What materials are hammer unions made from?

Hammer unions are typically manufactured from forged carbon steel, alloy steel, stainless steel, and NACE-compliant materials for sour gas service applications. Material selection depends on pressure, temperature, and corrosion requirements.

What is the difference between low, medium, and high-pressure hammer unions?

1. Low-pressure hammer unions use a metal-to-metal sealing design. 2. Medium-pressure hammer unions combine metal-to-metal sealing with an O-ring for enhanced sealing integrity. 3. High-pressure hammer unions use a lip-type seal ring for superior pressure containment.

Are hammer unions suitable for sour gas service?

Yes. Sour service hammer unions are manufactured using NACE-compliant materials to resist hydrogen sulfide (H2S) corrosion and sulfide stress cracking, making them safe for corrosive oilfield environments.

What type of end connection does Asian Sealing Products offer in their Hammer Union?

Asian Sealing Products Hammer unions will be available in both Threaded, and Butt-welded end connections. (May vary depending on the model). Materials and the model of the hammer union will change according to the above factors.

What sizes are available in hammer unions?

Hammer unions are commonly available in sizes ranging from 1 inch to 12 inches, depending on the application and pressure rating requirements.

Is there Colour coding done on Hammer Unions?

All models of hammer unions are color-coded; this allows quick identification on the field. All unions intended for sour gas service will be painted Olive green, stamped with “NACE MR-0175” or “SOUR GAS” on the body of the wing nut.

How often should hammer unions be inspected?

Hammer unions should be inspected before installation and periodically during operation. Regular inspection of threads, sealing surfaces, and components helps prevent leaks and ensures long-term reliability.

What safety precautions to follow when using hammer unions?

Hammer unions should always be tightened firmly to avoid any major leaks. It is always essential to pick the right hammer union for your application and pressure needs. When connecting and disconnecting the unions, the personnel should not use excessive force.
