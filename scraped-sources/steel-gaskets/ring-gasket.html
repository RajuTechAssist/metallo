Ring Gasket Manufacturer for High-Pressure RTJ Flange Sealing

A ring gasket, also known as a Ring Type Joint (RTJ) or Ring Joint gasket, is a precision-machined metallic sealing component designed for high-pressure and high-temperature flange connections in critical industrial piping systems. Installed inside specially machined flange grooves, the ring gasket compresses under bolt load to create a strong metal-to-metal seal, ensuring leak-proof performance even in extreme operating conditions.

We supply different ring gasket profiles including Type R (oval and octagonal), RX, and BX, suitable for various pressure classes and flange systems. All ring gaskets are manufactured in accordance with ASME B16.20, API 6A, DIN, and BS standards, ensuring dimensional accuracy, reliable sealing performance, and safe operation in critical industrial applications.

Product Details

Ring Type Joint Gaskets – Oval & Octagonal Profiles

Oval and octagonal cross-section Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are widely used in applications with operating pressures of up to 10,000 psi, providing reliable metal-to-metal sealing for critical flange connections.

All dimensions and groove tolerances for oval and octagonal RTJ gaskets are manufactured in strict accordance with API 6A and ASME B16.20 standards, ensuring accurate flange compatibility and consistent performance.

We maintain a comprehensive inventory of RTJ gaskets in a wide range of sizes and material grades, available for immediate dispatch from Ring Numbers R11 through R105.

Ring Joint Gaskets BX and SBX Type

BX-type Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are specifically engineered for very high-pressure applications, where reliable sealing under extreme operating conditions is critical. Each BX gasket incorporates a pressure balance hole designed to equalize any pressure that may become trapped within the flange grooves, thereby enhancing sealing stability and safety.

BX-type gaskets are exclusively compatible with API BX flanges and grooves and are available in standard sizes ranging from BX150 to BX303, in accordance with applicable API specifications.

SBX-type RTJ gaskets are manufactured in compliance with the API 17D standard for subsea and critical offshore applications. While the dimensions and tolerances of SBX gaskets follow API 6A requirements, they include an additional pressure passage hole, provided in two configuration options as specified under API 17D, depending on the installation and service requirements.

To meet stringent subsea performance and durability criteria, SBX-type gaskets are manufactured exclusively from non-corrosive materials, as mandated by API 17D, ensuring long-term reliability in aggressive offshore environments.

Ring Joint Gaskets RX Type

RX-type Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are an enhanced adaptation of the standard Type R gasket, engineered to deliver improved sealing performance under higher internal pressures. While maintaining the same groove design as Type R gaskets, RX gaskets are fully interchangeable with standard R-type gaskets.

The RX profile is designed to become pressure-energized during operation, allowing the gasket to deform more effectively under increasing system pressure and achieve superior metal-to-metal sealing.

RX-type RTJ gaskets are available in standard sizes ranging from RX20 to RX215, in accordance with applicable API and ASME specifications.

Combination (Transition) Ring Gaskets

Combination rings, also known as transition ring gaskets, are specially designed to connect two flanges of different nominal sizes while maintaining the same pitch circle diameter, allowing proper bolt alignment and secure assembly.

These ring gaskets enable safe and reliable sealing between mismatched flange sizes, making them ideal for retrofit applications, system modifications, or temporary connections where standard flange matching is not possible. Transition rings provide an effective solution without compromising sealing integrity or joint performance in high-pressure service conditions.

Ring Type Joint Gaskets – SRX Type

SRX-type Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are similar in design and sealing performance to RX-type gaskets, but incorporate a special vent hole to allow trapped water to escape during installation and operation in subsea applications.

This venting feature helps prevent pressure buildup caused by trapped fluids, improving sealing reliability and reducing the risk of gasket or flange damage in underwater and offshore service conditions.

Ring Type Blind Gaskets

Blind Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are manufactured with a solid profile to fully isolate or blank off pipelines and pressure vessels during maintenance, testing, or shutdown operations. These gaskets provide secure sealing in high-pressure flange connections where flow isolation is required.

Rubber-coated RTJ gaskets, typically manufactured from steel with an elastomer coating, are commonly used during hydrostatic and pressure testing to minimize damage to flange grooves and sealing surfaces. The rubber coating creates additional sealing contact points while protecting the flange faces from metal-to-metal abrasion, ensuring safe testing without compromising flange integrity.

Ring Joint Gaskets – Kammprofile (Octagonal)

Kammprofile Octagonal (Kammpro-OCT) Ring Type Joint (RTJ) gaskets are manufactured to standard octagonal dimensions in accordance with API 6A and ASME B16.20.

The Kammprofile design incorporates precision-engineered grooves on the gasket surface, which are then faced with oxidation-inhibited flexible graphite to enhance sealing performance. This design is particularly effective in applications where flange groove cracking or embrittlement has been observed, providing a reliable seal while accommodating flange surface imperfections.

Kammpro-OCT gaskets combine the metal-to-metal sealing strength of RTJ gaskets with the flexibility and chemical resistance of graphite facings, making them ideal for high-pressure, high-temperature, and aggressive chemical environments.

Ring Type Joint Gaskets – Pressure-Energized (PEG)

Pressure-Energized Gaskets (PEG) with Kammprofiled surfaces are ideal for high-pressure reactors, often used at the top and bottom flanges of hydroprocessing equipment.

The serrated surfaces, faced with oxidation-inhibited flexible graphite, allow the graphite to flow into minor imperfections under compression, ensuring enhanced seal tightness and reliable performance in extreme conditions.

Ring Type Joint Gaskets – Kammprofile Adapter Gaskets

Kammprofile Adapter Gaskets enable RTJ flanges to mate with raised-face flanges, combining the strength of full-metal construction with Kammprofiled sealing surfaces laminated with flexible graphite.

With the groove pitch properly positioned under the raised face, this design provides one of the most robust and reliable adapter-style seals for high-pressure applications.

Bridgeman Gaskets

Bridgeman gaskets are pressure-activated gaskets commonly used on pressure vessel heads and valve bonnets for pressures of 1500 psi and above.

The gasket’s cross-section is designed so that internal pressure converts axial force into radial sealing force, creating a self-energized metal-to-metal seal.

To enhance performance, Bridgeman gaskets are often silver-plated or lead-plated, providing a softer surface that facilitates metal flow into the flange, reducing the force required for effective sealing.

Delta Gaskets

Delta gaskets are pressure-actuated gaskets designed for high-pressure applications, typically on pressure vessels and valve bonnets operating above 5000 psi.

Under internal pressure, the gasket material expands to maintain a tight seal, counteracting the forces that tend to separate the flanges.

As with lens gaskets, complete drawings and material specifications are required, and the flange surface must be finished to 63 microinches or smoother to ensure reliable sealing performance.

Lens Ring Gaskets

Lens ring gaskets are line-contact seals used in high-pressure piping systems and pressure vessel heads.

With a spherical cross-section, these gaskets require precisely machined flanges. Their small contact area allows seating with minimal bolt load, while generating high gasket pressure for effective sealing.

Gasket materials are typically softer than the flange, and complete drawings and material specifications must be provided when ordering to ensure proper fit and performance.

RTJ Kammprofile Gaskets

RTJ Kammprofile gaskets are composite gaskets featuring a serrated metal core with a soft facing material. The metal core is machined with concentric serrations on each contact face, creating high-pressure points that allow the soft facing to flow into flange imperfections, even at relatively low bolt loads.

The soft facing material, commonly expanded graphite, compresses into the serrations, forming a thin, uniform film across the peaks to achieve optimal sealing density within the groove.

This design combines the conformability of soft materials with the strength and reliability of metallic gaskets. Alternative facings such as PTFE (for chemically aggressive services) or mica (for high-temperature applications) can also be used, depending on the operating conditions.

Ring Gasket - A complete guide

Answers to all your possible questions.

How do I install an RTJ (Ring Joint) gasket?

Clean flange grooves and the gasket thoroughly. Inspect for burrs or pits. Center the ring in the groove, align flanges vertically, and tighten bolts in a star pattern to ensure even compression.

What are Ring Gaskets (RTJ gaskets) and how do they work?

Ring gaskets also known as RTJ (Ring Type Joint) gaskets, are solid metal sealing rings used in high-pressure and high-temperature applications. They are specifically designed to fit into the ring groove of RTJ flanges. When the flange bolts are tightened, the RTJ gasket is compressed into the groove, creating a metal-to-metal seal. This tight seal prevents leakage even under extreme pressure and temperature conditions, making RTJ gaskets ideal for oil & gas, petrochemical, power generation, and offshore applications.

How do I select the right RTJ gasket for my application?

Choosing the correct Ring gasket (RTJ ) depends on your flange type, pressure class, and operating conditions. Our experts recommend reviewing flange dimensions, material compatibility, and temperature/pressure requirements to ensure optimal performance and safety.

How do I troubleshoot leakage issues with Ring gaskets?

Leakage can result from misalignment, flange surface imperfections, or incorrect torque. Check for proper installation, flange surface condition, and ensure the gasket material is suitable for the operating environment. Our technical team can assist with advanced troubleshooting.

How often should RTJ gaskets be replaced?

The lifespan of RTJ gaskets depends on various factors, such as operating conditions, maintenance practices, and gasket material. Regular inspections of the gaskets should be performed, and they should be replaced if signs of wear, degradation, or loss of sealing effectiveness are observed.

What pressure classes and flange standards are compatible with RTJ gaskets?

 RTJ (Ring Type Joint) gaskets are designed for high-pressure and high-temperature applications and are commonly used with ASME/ANSI B16.5 and API 6A flanges. They are compatible with pressure classes ranging from Class 150 up to Class 2500, depending on the gasket type and material.
