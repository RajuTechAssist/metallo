Insulation Gasket Materials

Asian Sealing Products manufactures flange insulation gasket using carefully selected insulating materials to suit different pressure classes, temperatures, and chemical environments. Our insulation gasket solutions are produced using both non-metallic and laminated composite materials, selected based on application requirements.

The choice of insulation gasket material plays a critical role in sealing performance, electrical isolation, mechanical strength, and long-term durability, especially in high-pressure and corrosive service conditions.

Ring gaskets are manufactured using various materials based on the application’s requirements. The material selection depends on factors such as the type of fluids being sealed, operating conditions (including temperature and pressure), and the overall environment. Here are some common materials used for manufacturing ring gaskets:

Phenolic

Phenolic insulation gaskets offer good mechanical strength and electrical insulation, making them suitable for general industrial applications. They are commonly used in low to medium-pressure flange connections.

Neoprene-Faced Phenolic

This material combines a phenolic core with a neoprene sealing surface, providing improved sealing performance and resistance to minor flange surface irregularities. It is widely used in flange insulation gasket kits where enhanced sealing reliability is required.

G10 / G11 Glass Epoxy

G10 and G11 insulation gaskets provide high mechanical strength, excellent electrical insulation, and superior temperature resistance. These materials are ideal for high-pressure and high-temperature applications in oil & gas and petrochemical industries.

Glass Reinforced Epoxy (GRE)

GRE insulation gaskets offer excellent chemical resistance and dimensional stability, making them suitable for aggressive service conditions and corrosive environments.

PTFE

PTFE insulation gaskets provide outstanding chemical resistance and low-friction properties. They are commonly used in applications involving aggressive chemicals or where high purity is required.

Custom Composite Materials

We also offer custom insulation gasket materials tailored to specific operating conditions, flange standards, and customer requirements.

Product Details

What Does a Complete Insulation Gasket Kit from Asian Sealing Products Include?

A complete insulation gasket kit from Asian Sealing Products is designed to provide reliable flange isolation, corrosion protection, and leak-free sealing in a single, ready-to-install package. Each flange insulation gasket kit is engineered for performance, durability, and ease of installation in industrial piping systems.

Standard Components of an Insulation Gasket Kit

  • Insulating Gasket
    Precision-engineered insulation gasket designed to deliver effective sealing and electrical isolation between flanged connections.
  • Insulating Sleeves
    Non-conductive sleeves installed over the bolts to prevent metal-to-metal contact and eliminate electrical current flow.
  • Insulating Washers
    High-strength, non-conductive washers placed on either side of the flange to maintain complete electrical isolation.
  • Metal Backup Washers
    Steel backup washers that provide mechanical support and protect insulating washers during bolt tightening.

Materials and Customization Options

Each flange insulation gasket kit is manufactured using high-quality, corrosion-resistant materials such as G10, G11, PTFE, and epoxy-coated metals, selected based on pressure, temperature, and chemical exposure.

ASP insulation kits are available in Type F (Full Face), Type E, and Type D configurations and can be customized to suit ANSI, DIN, ASME, and other international flange specifications.

TYPE E (Full Face Type Gasket) Insulation Gasket

  • Complete electrical isolation
  • Enhanced sealing coverage across the entire flange face, including flange bolts
  • Prevents flange face corrosion and fluid leakage
  • Comes with the same outside diameter as the flanges. Hence, the foreign material collection between the flange faces is prevented
  • Ideal for high-pressure and corrosive environments
  • Suitable for flat face and raised face flanges
  • The E gaskets are available in plain-faced Phenolic, Neoprene-faced Phenolic, and high temperature material like G-10 that offer excellent mechanical strength, chemical resistance, and thermal stability.

Choose TYPE E when your application demands maximum coverage and comprehensive isolation protection.

TYPE F Insulation Gasket

  • Designed to fit only the raised face area of flanges
  • Provides reliable sealing and electrical insulation
  • The outside diameter of the gasket is slightly less than the inside diameter of the bolt circle
  • For best results, the flanges must be wrapped with Flange Band Protectors. So foreign materials are prevented from collecting and creating a bridge, thus shorting out the isolation
  • Ideal for high-pressure pipeline systems
  • Prevents galvanic corrosion between dissimilar metals

Choose TYPE F Insulation Gaskets when you need a compact, high-efficiency sealing solution tailored for raised face flange designs.

Type D (RTJ Type Gaskets)

  • Precisely fits RTJ flange grooves for optimal sealing
  • Provides strong electrical insulation and mechanical strength
  • Ideal for extreme pressure and temperature environments
  • Prevents leakage and flange corrosion
  • Available in Phenolic and G10 material types
  • Suitable for critical oil & gas, petrochemical, and power industry applications

For a robust, leak-proof, and electrically isolating solution in RTJ flange systems, TYPE D Insulation Gaskets deliver trusted performance and durability.

Answers to all your possible questions.

What is an insulation gasket?

An insulation gasket is a sealing component installed between flanged joints to provide electrical isolation, prevent galvanic corrosion, and maintain a leak-tight seal in piping systems.

What is a flange insulation gasket used for?

A flange insulation gasket is used to isolate flanged connections in pipelines, preventing electrical current flow between dissimilar metals and reducing the risk of corrosion and leakage.

What is included in a flange insulation gasket kit?

A standard flange insulation gasket kit includes an insulation gasket, insulating sleeves, insulating washers, and steel backing washers required for proper flange isolation and installation.

Which flange standards are supported for insulation gasket kits?

ASP flange insulation gasket kits are manufactured to suit ANSI, API, DIN, BS, AS, and MSSP standards, including ASME B16.5 Raised Face (RF), Full Face (FF), and RTJ flanges.

What materials are used for insulation gaskets?

Insulation gaskets are made from non-metallic and laminated composite materials such as phenolic, neoprene-faced phenolic, G10/G11 glass epoxy, GRE, and PTFE, depending on operating conditions.

How do I select the right insulation gasket material?

Selection depends on pressure rating, temperature, chemical exposure, flange type, and service conditions. ASP provides technical support to help choose the correct material.

Are flange insulation gasket kits suitable for high-pressure applications?

Yes. Kits manufactured using laminated composite materials like G10/G11 are suitable for high-pressure and high-temperature industrial applications.

How thick is an insulation gasket?

Thickness depends on flange standard, pressure class, and application requirements. Common thicknesses range from 1/16 inch (1.6 mm) to 1/4 inch (6.4 mm), with custom options available.

How do F, E, D, and O gasket isolation kits differ?

F, E, D, and O gasket isolation kits differ in terms of their design and construction- the geometry, materials, and thickness of the insulating components. Each type is designed to meet specific industry standards and requirements, with varying levels of electrical insulation and corrosion resistance. (1) F-Type (Full Face) covers the entire flange face, including bolt holes. (2) E-Type fits inside the bolt circle (3) D-Type insulates the bore and flange face without bolt hole coverage. (4) O-Type provides bore-only insulation for specific applications

Can flange insulation gasket kits be customized?

Yes. ASP offers custom flange insulation gasket kits based on flange type and size, pressure class, material requirements, and application specifications.
