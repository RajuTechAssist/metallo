PTFE and CNAF Gasket Materials

PTFE and CNAF gaskets are manufactured using specialized materials selected according to operating conditions, fluid compatibility, temperature, and pressure requirements. These materials are engineered to provide strong compression, thermal stability, and leak-free sealing performance in demanding industrial environments. The choice of gasket material plays a critical role in ensuring reliable sealing performance, chemical resistance, and durability in industrial applications.

Ring gaskets are manufactured using various materials based on the application’s requirements. The material selection depends on factors such as the type of fluids being sealed, operating conditions (including temperature and pressure), and the overall environment. Here are some common materials used for manufacturing ring gaskets:

Virgin PTFE

Virgin PTFE offers excellent chemical resistance, low friction, and non-stick properties, making it ideal for highly corrosive environments and high-purity applications such as chemical processing, pharmaceuticals, and food production.

Glass-Filled PTFE

Glass-filled PTFE improves compressive strength and reduces creep (cold flow), providing better dimensional stability and sealing performance in high-pressure industrial applications.

Carbon-Filled PTFE

Carbon-filled PTFE enhances thermal conductivity and wear resistance while reducing deformation under load, making it suitable for high-temperature and high-pressure sealing environments.

Expanded PTFE (ePTFE)

Expanded PTFE features a flexible micro-porous structure that provides excellent compressibility and sealing capability, especially on irregular or damaged flange surfaces.

Aramid Fibres

Aramid fibres provide high tensile strength and heat resistance, serving as the primary reinforcement material in CNAF gasket sheets to ensure durability and pressure resistance.

Mineral Fillers

Mineral fillers improve density, thermal stability, and resistance to pressure, enhancing the overall performance of compressed non-asbestos fibre gaskets.

Elastomer Binders

Elastomer binders act as bonding agents in CNAF gasket materials, providing flexibility, compressibility, and reliable sealing integrity under varying operating conditions.

PTFE Gasket Types and Their Properties

PTFE Gasket solutions are designed for high-performance industrial applications that require chemical resistance, temperature stability, and pressure endurance. This PTFE gasket is manufactured in Virgin PTFE, Carbon-Filled PTFE, and Glass-Filled PTFE grades and offers low creep, high dielectric properties, and long service life across a wide range of operating conditions.

Raised-Face PTFE Gaskets

These PTFE Gaskets are designed to be used in high-pressure flange joints (high-pressure) where uniform distribution of loads is necessary, and leak-free sealing is required.

Full-Face PTFE Gaskets

These PTFE Gaskets are used on flat-face flanges, and they cover the whole surface of the flange, eliminate flange damage, and offer uniform chemical resistance.

Custom PTFE Gaskets

PTFE gasket solutions for non-standard flanges and equipment are manufactured in custom-cut forms to meet specific mechanical and sealing requirements.

PTFE Envelope Gaskets

This PTFE gasket combines a compressible core with chemical protection, making the gasket suitable for aggressive media under moderate pressure and temperature conditions.

Expanded PTFE Gaskets (ePTFE)

The ePTFE Gaskets have a micro-fibrous design that provides high compressibility, low creep, and reliable sealing of irregular or damaged flanges, with temperatures ranging between -200 °C and 260 °C.

CNAF Gasket Types and Their Properties

The CNAF Gaskets (Compressed Non-Asbestos Fibre Gaskets) are applied in tight compressibility as well as cost-efficient sealing. These non-asbestos gaskets are made using aramid fibres, glass fibres, and elastomeric binders to ensure a good service in thermal and pressure-based systems.

Aramid Fibre with NBR Binder

Applicable in the oil, fuel, and hydrocarbon services, it provides stability and consistency in sealing under pressure.

Glass Fibre with NBR Binder

A cost-effective CNAF Gasket solution with good compression recovery in a moderate temperature and pressure environment.

SBR/NR Binder CNAF Gaskets

Applicable best when flexibility and low installation cost are needed, and steam, water, and boiler sealing are required.

Reinforced Graphite Gaskets

Graphite gaskets are reinforced with a view to dealing with harsh pressure and temperature conditions. They are designed using expanded graphite, reinforced with stainless steel inserts; they are very chemically resistant and stable in dimensions. These gaskets are typically applied together with PTFE Gaskets and CNAF Gaskets in intricate sealing systems that deal with steam, oil, and chemical processing.

Answers to all your possible questions.

What are PTFE gaskets made of?

PTFE gaskets are manufactured from Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE), a high-performance fluoropolymer known for its exceptional chemical resistance, low friction, and high-temperature stability. Depending on the application, PTFE gasket materials may also include glass-filled PTFE, carbon-filled PTFE, or expanded PTFE (ePTFE) to improve strength, reduce creep, and enhance sealing performance.

What applications are PTFE and CNAF gaskets suitable for?

PTFE gaskets are suitable for chemical processing, petrochemical plants, oil and gas pipelines, food and pharmaceutical industries, and high-temperature or corrosive environments. CNAF gaskets are commonly used in steam lines, boilers, water systems, compressors, and general industrial sealing applications where mechanical strength and controlled compression are required.

What is cold flow and how does it affect PTFE gaskets?

Cold flow, also known as creep, is the tendency of PTFE material to deform gradually under constant load. In flange applications, this may reduce sealing stress over time. Using filled PTFE grades, such as glass- or carbon-filled PTFE, helps minimize cold flow and improve long-term sealing reliability.

. What is the difference between PTFE and CNAF gaskets in industrial applications?

PTFE gaskets offer superior chemical resistance and perform well in extreme temperatures, making them ideal for corrosive environments. CNAF gaskets are better suited for steam, water, and oil applications where strength, durability, and controlled compression are required.

What temperature range can PTFE gaskets withstand?

PTFE gaskets can operate within a temperature range of approximately -200°C to +260°C, depending on operating pressure and application conditions.

Are PTFE gaskets FDA approved?

Yes, selected grades of PTFE gaskets are FDA-compliant and safe for use in food processing, beverage production, and pharmaceutical manufacturing where contamination-free sealing is required.

Are CNAF gaskets asbestos-free?

Yes, CNAF stands for Compressed Non-Asbestos Fibre. These gaskets are manufactured using aramid fibres and elastomers, providing safe and environmentally compliant sealing solutions.

Which gasket is better for chemical applications?

PTFE gaskets are generally better for chemical applications because of their excellent resistance to corrosive media, acids, and solvents.

Do you supply custom-sized PTFE and CNAF gaskets?

Yes, PTFE and CNAF gaskets can be manufactured in custom sizes and flange standards to meet specific industrial sealing requirements.

How do I choose the right gasket thickness?

The correct gasket thickness depends on flange condition, pressure rating, bolt load, and operating temperature. Thinner gaskets are preferred for high-pressure systems, while slightly thicker gaskets may compensate for minor flange imperfections. Proper engineering evaluation ensures optimal sealing performance.

What media are PTFE and CNAF gaskets not suitable for?

PTFE gaskets are generally not recommended for molten alkali metals or certain fluorinating agents at high temperatures. CNAF gaskets may not be suitable for highly aggressive acids or extreme chemical environments where pure PTFE offers better resistance. Always verify chemical compatibility before selection.

Yes, we are a PTFE gasket and CNAF gasket manufacturer in India, supplying precision-cut industrial gaskets as per ASME, DIN, and IS standards for domestic and international projects.
