Instrumentation Flanges

Orifice Flanges

Engineered with precision flow tappings for accurate metering of liquids and gases.

SOTCO Orifice Flanges are used in conjunction with orifice meters to measure flow rates in pipelines. Unlike standard flanges, they feature pairs of pressure tappings machined directly into the flange ring. This eliminates the need for separate orifice carriers or pipe wall drilling.

Key Feature: Every set comes with a **Jack Screw** mechanism. This allows for safe and easy spreading of the flanges to inspect or replace the Orifice Plate and gaskets without springing the pipeline.

Technical Specifications

 Design Standard ASME B16.36 (Dimensions & Tolerances for Orifice Flanges)
Available Sizes 1″ to 24″ (Standard Coverage)
Pressure Class Coverage Weld Neck: Class 300, 400, 600, 900, 1500, 2500
Slip-On / Threaded: Class 300 Only
Tapping Connections 2x 1/2" NPT Tappings (180° apart) per flange. Plugged on request.
Facing Types Raised Face (RF) - Smooth or Serrated, Ring Type Joint (RTJ)
Hardware Supplied with Jack Screws (for spreading) and Precision Plugs.
