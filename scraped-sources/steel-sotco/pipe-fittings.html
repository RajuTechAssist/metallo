Pipe Fittings

  • Home

  • Pipe Fittings

High-integrity Butt Weld Fittings for seamless piping connections.

SOTCO Pipe Fittings are engineered to change direction, branch off, or change the diameter of a piping system. Manufactured to **ASME B16.9** standards, our fittings ensure smooth flow characteristics and high structural strength. Whether for high-pressure oil lines or critical chemical processes, our fittings provide leak-proof, permanent connections.

B16.9 Standard
Seamless Construction
100% Radiography
