Socket Weld Flanges (SWF)

Designed for high-pressure small-bore piping where smooth fluid flow is critical.

The SOTCO Socket Weld Flange features a unique counter-bore that accepts the pipe before welding. Unlike slip-on flanges, the bore of the flange matches the ID of the pipe, eliminating internal turbulence and erosion.

Key Advantage: They are attached by inserting the pipe into the socket end and applying a fillet weld on the top hub. This design is extensively used in high-pressure hydraulic systems and steam lines where joint strength and flow efficiency are paramount.
