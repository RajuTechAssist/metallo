Forged Fittings

  • Forged Fittings

High-strength Forged Fittings for critical high-pressure piping systems.

SOTCO Forged Fittings (Socket Weld & Threaded) are manufactured to **ASME B16.11** standards, designed to withstand extreme pressure and vibration. Unlike butt-weld fittings, these are forged from solid blocks of steel, ensuring superior grain structure and mechanical strength. They are indispensable in small-bore piping for oil & gas, petrochemical, and power generation industries.
