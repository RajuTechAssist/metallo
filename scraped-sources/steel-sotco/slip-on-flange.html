Slip-On Flanges (SORF)

Engineered for precision alignment and cost-efficiency in low-to-medium pressure piping systems.

The SOTCO Slip-On Flange is the preferred choice for engineers requiring flexibility during fabrication. Unlike Weld Neck flanges, the pipe slides into the Slip-On flange before welding, eliminating the need for cutting pipes to exact length.

Key Engineering Benefit: These flanges are double-welded (both inside and outside) to ensure joint integrity and prevent leakage. They are widely used in cooling water lines, firefighting systems, and low-pressure petrochemical applications where ease of assembly is critical.
