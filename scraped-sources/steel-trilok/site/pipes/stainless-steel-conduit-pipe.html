What is Stainless Steel Conduit Pipe?
A stainless steel conduit is a heavy-duty galvanized tube that can be installed with threaded fittings. These pipes are very flexible and are utilized to provide protection to electrical wiring in a structure or building components. These pipes are very rigid and provide support to different applications.

What are stainless steel conduits used for?
A conduit pipe is used to provide protection from damage to electrical cables, and panels. They can also be used to add an aesthetical appeal to a wide spectrum of homes or commercial spaces.

What is BS 4568 Stainless Steel Conduit Pipe?
The BS 4568 conduit pipe is a high quality galvanized pipe that has a smooth surface finish. These pipes have a aesthetic wall surface without any spots or bubbles. They are available in lengths up to 4 meters and can be availed in sizes up to 32mm. The conduit pipes can be used alongside mechanical equipment for protective wire installation.

What is AISI 316L Rigid Stainless Steel Conduit?
The rigid 316L grade is a low carbon version of the stainless steel component. The rigid modules are heavy-duty galvanized modules that are typically installed with threaded fittings. They provide protection against damage and provide structural support to different applications.

What is SS 304 Conduit Tube?
The SS 304 conduit tube is a high strength grade that is designed with a superior composition of nickel and chromium, giving these tubes enhanced corrosion and oxidation resistance properties. These tubes are capable of withstanding harsh conditions like humidity, extreme heat, or salinity.

AISI 316L rigid stainless steel conduit Features

High Corrosion Resistance

Food processing lines, chemical facilities, water treatment plants, seaside operations, and other corrosive environments benefit from stainless steel (SUS304) construction..

IMC Conduit Compliant

Internal diameter and length meet IMC specifications. In a number of applications, it can be used with steel conduit for a more flexible and dependable electrical installation. Stainless conduit fittings aid in the creation of a professional-looking wiring system.

Long Life Expectancy

Wherever they are installed, conduit systems must stay in good working order. Stainless steel conduit has a long service life and requires little maintenance, which is especially important in high-altitude applications.

Outstanding Appearance

For a greater appearance, stainless steel conduit has been polished to a bright gloss. This ensures a pleasing appearance, which is particularly important for food processing operations.

Standards of Electrical Metallic Tubing

Conduit EMT pipe is designed in accordance with the most recent edition of the following specifications.

  • Rigid Steel EMT (ANSI® C80.3) is an American National Standard.

  • EMT-Steel Underwriters Laboratories Standard (UL797)

  • Article 358 of the National Electric Code® (NEC®) of 2002 (1999 NEC® Article 348)
