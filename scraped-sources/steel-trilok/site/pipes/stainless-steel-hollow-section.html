ASTM A554 Gr 304 Hollow Section and SS 316 Circular Hollow Tube Exporter in India


A hollow section metal covers the profile for a hollow cross-sectional area. Stainless Steel Hollow Pipe is a high-strength grade that is produced in a cold or hot forming process. These pipes are structural steel applications that provide good stability and torsion to different modules. The lightweight grade is very durable and can be produced in different shapes and sizes. We are the Stainless Steel Hollow Section Supplier who believes in quality over anything. Our esteemed workers make use of the latest technology and best raw materials to produce top-of-line products. These sections we produce can be custom designed in different shapes and sizes to meet different product requirements.

The ASTM A554 Gr 304 Rectangular Hollow Tube is designed with superior mechanical properties and enhanced corrosion resistance properties. Designed in a rectangular cross section these tubes are designed in different finishes and can be used for multiple purposes. ASTM A554 Gr 304L Rectangular Hollow Section possesses a lower content of carbon than the 304 hollow sections. These tubes are resistant to sensitization and prevent grade boundary formation when they are welded in place. The SS 316 circular hollow Pipe is cold worked and can be welded or bolted in place. These pipes have a superior chemical composition and showcase enhanced resistance to corrosive and oxidative media. The low carbon ASTM A554 Tp 316L Seamless Hollow Tube is manufactured without the need for welds or seams. These tubes have enhanced strength and outstanding load-bearing capacity across stress-induced environments.
