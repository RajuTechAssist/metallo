Stainless Steel Seamless Tube and SS Welded Tubing Stockist in Mumbai


Stainless steel is a sought after ferrous steel grade. A Stainless Steel Tube is manufactured with a chemical composition of over 11% of chromium alongside other alloying components. This content gives the tubes excellent resistance against corrosive and oxidative media in the system. The Stainless Steel Tubing exhibits outstanding strength and good mechanical properties. This tubing can be designed in square, rectangular, and circular cross sections. A Stainless Steel Round Tube is the most common shape of these tubes. These tubes support different applications across harsh environmental conditions.

The Stainless Steel Seamless Tube is designed by passing hot steel billets through extruded metal and precisely shaping them in place. These tubes showcase enhanced strength and excellent load-bearing capacity. Stainless Steel Welded Tube is produced as sheets or strips are rolled followed by welding them in place utilizing standard shop welding procedures. The Stainless Steel Tube is a lightweight module that is used to hold and withstand different types of forces and pressures across an array of stress-induced setups. These tubes are available in different schedules. A Schedule 10 Stainless Steel Tube is utilized for low-pressure systems as its load-bearing capacity is less. They are generally used in a system where stress is low. A Stainless Steel Thick Wall Tube, on the contrary, has walls that allow it to carry and transport high pressure and temperature fluids and gases more easily in the system. The tubes can be processed in hot or cold drawing procedures. A Cold Drawn Stainless Steel Tube is a high-strength grade that exhibits excellent surface finish and has superior corrosion resistance properties. These tubes can be customized in different shapes and sizes to meet project requirements. If you have any queries and questions about our products or their pricing, contact us for more information.
