304 Polish Pipe and ASTM A554 Gr 304L Polished Tubing Stockist In Mumbai


A polished tube is an aesthetically appealing grade that is utilized in different applications. The versatile Polished Stainless Steel Pipe is polished till there are no ridges or scratches on the surface of the pipe. This reduces corrosion build-up and adds a glow to the piping components. This pipe allows for a smooth passage of flow of fluids and gases within the system. A Stainless Steel Polished tubing has both internal and outer radius corners. The tubes can be finished in an array of surface types namely, mirror, electropolished, and so on. The ASTM A554 Gr 304 Polished Pipe is designed with a smooth surface finish. These pipes possess superior composition of chromium and nickel with other constituent items. This gives them excellent corrosion and oxidation resistance properties. They are utilized in cosmetics, food processing, pharmaceutical, vacuum systems, etc. The ASTM A554 Gr 304L Polished Tubing is a low-carbon grade that resists sensitization or formation of grain boundary on the tubes after welding procedures.
