Technology

The high-quality, sturdy stainless steel that our clients receive come straight from state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities. Our production facilities are laden with modern, technologically-advanced and sophisticated equipment -

  • Tube Mills
  • Pilgher Lines
  • Draw Benches
  • Heat treatment furnace
  • Mechanical & Chemical Laboratory
  • Modernistic Finishing & Inspection Lines

All the processes are carried out under a single roof which ensures that the products are manufactured and delivered on time. We customize the work schedule of our employees to suit the customized requirements of our clients. Our Company adheres to strict quality check procedures, the inspection of which begins from the designing stage right up to the production.

Quality

We ensure that all our products meet the highest quality standards. The procedures of Quality control are properly identified, documented and implemented. Our team of supervisors and quality control inspectors check the Tolerance, Length, and Ovality at various stages of production.

Our certifications such as ISO 9001-2015 approved by TÜV NORD, AD 2000 Merkblatt WO approved by TÜV NORD, Pressure Equipment Directive - PED 97/23/EC approved by TÜV NORD confirm that quality is at the heart of our manufacturing processes.

Hindustan is an ISO 14000:2005 and ISO 9001 2015 certified organization. The industry has rewarded us with the following certifications

  • AD 2000 MERKBLATT W0
  • BS OHSAS 18001:2007
  • PED 97 23 EC
