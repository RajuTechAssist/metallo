Fasteners Manufacturer in India

Fasteners Manufacturer in India - American Stainless

American Stainless is one of India's finest Fasteners Manufacturers, Suppliers, and Dealers. Our company is situated in India and specializes in the production and export of Fasteners. We are your one-stop shop for all of your Fasteners requirements. We specialize in manufacturing high-quality Fasteners for the automotive, aerospace, shipbuilding, and power plant industries.

Due to our vast inventory, leading manufacturing and supplier of high-quality metal products, we have satisfied many customer requirements. Our business has been established for a long time and has experienced remarkable growth. Fasteners come in a variety of grades, diameters, and sizes.

Stainless Steel Fasteners Manufacturer, Carbon Steel Fasteners Supplier, Copper Fasteners Exporter in India.

American Stainless produces a variety of metal products, including Fasteners. We are a fabricator of raw materials and a final product company. We manufacture and export Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Hastelloy, Inconel, Titanium, Monel, Nickel Alloy, and Alloy Steel products in a variety of forms and sizes to meet our clients' needs.

Throughout the production and distribution operations, including the selection of high-quality raw materials, processing, labelling, packing, storage, and shipment, we have a skilled crew that ensures the quality of Fasteners and other Products. We are also a provider of Sheet, Plate & Coil.
